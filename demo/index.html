THE EXPERIMENTAL LESSON

The agent boundary
can be the bug.

The first design used an Impact Scout, Adversarial Reviewer and Evidence Verifier. It looked sophisticated—and performed worse than one tool-using reviewer.

Trajectory analysis showed lossy handoffs. The final product removes the swarm and gives one investigator the evidence it needs to prove a change-induced counterexample.
